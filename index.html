      Dynamics of Neuromuscular Fatigue Across Climbing-Specific Grips: An sEMG-Based Comparative Analysis

      • Designed and independently ran a pilot study using surface EMG to compare fatigue across crimp, half-crimp and sloper grips under a controlled exhaustion protocol

      • Processed and analysed EMG data in R (RStudio), cross-validated in Jamovi
      • Built and deployed an interactive HTML data explorer of the fatigue trajectories, linked by QR code in the final report
